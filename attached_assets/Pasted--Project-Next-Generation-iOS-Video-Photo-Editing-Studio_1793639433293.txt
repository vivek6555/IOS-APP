# Project: Next-Generation iOS Video & Photo Editing Studio

Act as a Senior iOS Product Architect, Professional Video Editor Engineer, Computational Photography Specialist, and UI/UX Designer.

I want to plan and eventually build a powerful, premium-quality iOS application that combines professional video editing, photo editing, social media content creation, and a professional camera into one application.

The goal is to create an application that delivers a polished, powerful creative experience comparable to leading professional editing applications, while having its own unique identity, interface, and workflow.

**IMPORTANT: Do not start coding immediately. First discuss the product vision, technical feasibility, architecture, features, development roadmap, and limitations with me. Challenge weak ideas and suggest better approaches.**

## 1. Product Vision

Build an all-in-one iOS Creative Studio with four major modules:

### A. Professional Video Editor
A powerful timeline-based video editor with:

- Multi-track video and audio timeline.
- Trim, split, crop, rotate, merge and speed adjustment.
- Slow motion, fast motion and reverse playback.
- Keyframe animation.
- Transitions and cinematic effects.
- Color grading, exposure, contrast, saturation, highlights and shadows.
- LUT support.
- Chroma key / green screen.
- Masking and blending modes.
- Text, animated titles, captions and subtitles.
- Music, voiceovers and sound effects.
- Audio waveform and volume control.
- Picture-in-picture.
- Video stabilization where technically supported.
- Background removal where feasible.
- High-quality video export.
- Project saving and editing continuation.

### B. Professional Photo Editor

Create a powerful photo-editing workspace featuring:

- Crop, resize, rotate and perspective correction.
- Exposure, brightness, contrast and color adjustments.
- HSL color controls.
- Curves and professional color grading.
- Filters and customizable presets.
- AI background removal.
- Object removal and retouching.
- Portrait enhancement.
- Blur and depth effects.
- Text, stickers, shapes and overlays.
- Layers and masking.
- Before/after comparison.
- High-resolution image export.

Preserve original image quality and avoid destructive editing wherever possible.

### C. Instagram & Social Media Content Studio

Make content creation for Instagram and other social platforms extremely convenient.

Include:

- Instagram Reels creation.
- Instagram Stories creation.
- Instagram Posts.
- Carousel designs.
- YouTube Shorts.
- TikTok-style vertical videos.
- Ready-made templates.
- Animated text and subtitles.
- Auto captions.
- Music synchronization.
- Beat detection.
- Social media aspect ratio presets:
  - 9:16
  - 4:5
  - 1:1
  - 16:9
- Platform-specific export presets.
- Cover and thumbnail creation.
- Brand kits and reusable templates.

The user should be able to import media, choose a format, edit, preview and export quickly.

### D. Professional Camera App

This should be a major standalone module, not just a basic camera screen.

Explore the possibility of building a professional manual camera with:

**Photo controls**
- Manual focus.
- Manual ISO.
- Shutter speed.
- White balance.
- Exposure compensation.
- Focus peaking if supported.
- Histogram.
- Grid and level indicators.
- RAW / ProRAW capture where supported.
- High-resolution capture.
- Timer and burst mode.
- Manual lens selection where supported.

**Video controls**
- Manual focus.
- ISO and shutter speed.
- White balance.
- Frame rate selection.
- 24, 25, 30, 60 and higher FPS where supported.
- Resolution selection, including 4K where supported.
- Video stabilization options.
- Focus and exposure locking.
- Audio input selection where supported.
- Live recording information.
- Professional recording interface.

Explore AVFoundation and native iOS camera APIs to determine which features are realistically available on different iPhone models.

## 2. Advanced AI Features

Investigate useful AI-powered features:

- Automatic video editing.
- Smart highlight detection.
- Automatic captions.
- Background removal.
- Object removal.
- AI image enhancement.
- AI-powered color correction.
- Automatic beat synchronization.
- Text-to-video possibilities.
- Image-to-video possibilities.
- Voice cleanup.
- Intelligent scene detection.
- AI-generated social media templates.

Separate features that can run on-device from features requiring cloud AI services. Explain API costs, privacy implications and processing requirements.

## 3. Premium UI/UX Requirements

The application should feel like a premium professional creative product.

Design principles:

- Modern, minimal and elegant interface.
- Dark editing workspace.
- Excellent typography and spacing.
- Smooth transitions and animations.
- Professional timeline interface.
- Intuitive gestures.
- Responsive editing controls.
- Native iOS interaction patterns.
- High-quality preview player.
- No unnecessary clutter.
- Consistent design system across all modules.

Create a distinctive brand identity rather than copying an existing application's interface.

## 4. Technical Architecture

Evaluate the best native iOS architecture.

Consider:

- Swift.
- SwiftUI.
- UIKit where necessary.
- AVFoundation.
- Core Image.
- Core Video.
- Metal.
- Photos framework.
- Vision framework.
- Core ML.
- Video encoding and decoding.
- Background rendering and export.
- Efficient memory management.
- GPU acceleration.
- Non-destructive editing architecture.
- Project file format and autosave.
- Media asset management.

Replit will be used for AI-assisted development and project planning. Clearly explain which parts can be developed through Replit and which parts require Xcode, macOS, an actual iPhone, or native iOS development tools.

Do not assume that a web application wrapped in a WebView will provide the required professional camera and editing capabilities.

## 5. Performance Requirements

The app must be designed for real-world professional usage.

Priorities:

- Smooth timeline playback.
- Efficient handling of 4K video.
- Responsive scrubbing.
- Low memory consumption.
- Reliable background export.
- No loss of editing projects.
- Efficient GPU processing.
- Good battery and thermal management.
- Support for different iPhone generations.

Explain what performance targets are realistic.
